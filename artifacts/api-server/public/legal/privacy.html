Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 20, 2026  ·  terra-call.com/legal/privacy

Terra Call ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Terra Call mobile application ("App") and the Terra Call SMS messaging program.

By using Terra Call, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

SMS Messaging Program — Mobile Information Disclosure

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and SMS delivery (Twilio), is permitted. All the categories below exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

This means your phone number and SMS opt-in status are never sold, rented, leased, traded, or shared with marketers, advertisers, data brokers, lead generators, or any third party for any purpose other than delivering the alert messages you have explicitly consented to receive within Terra Call.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Terra Call:

  • Full name and email address (provided during registration)
  • Phone number (provided in your profile settings)
  • Device location/GPS coordinates (for caregiver monitoring map)
  • Check-in timestamps and history
  • Role information (Care Recipient or Caregiver)
  • Emergency contact relationships between users
  • Device information (device type, operating system version)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide real-time check-in monitoring and emergency alerts
  • Send push notifications and SMS alerts to caregivers when a check-in is missed
  • Display care recipient location and status on the caregiver dashboard
  • Enable caregiver and care recipient account linking
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

Terra Call does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With linked caregivers or care recipients — your status, name, and phone number are visible to users you are connected with in the app
  • With Twilio — to send SMS alert messages to caregiver phone numbers
  • With Firebase (Google) — for authentication, data storage, and push notifications
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of our users

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud-based database service. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), Firebase Authentication for secure login, and role-based access controls so users only see data they are authorized to view.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@terra-call.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Location Data

Terra Call accesses your device location to display care recipient location on the caregiver monitoring map. Location data is only shared with caregivers who are linked to your account. You may disable location access in your device settings, though this will limit certain app features.

7. Camera Access (Future Feature)

A future version of Terra Call will include optional camera activation during emergencies. Care recipients will be asked to provide explicit consent at registration before this feature is enabled. Camera access will only occur during active emergency situations and only to linked caregivers.

8. Children's Privacy

Terra Call is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of SMS alerts by replying STOP to any message
  • Disable location access through your device settings

10. Third-Party Services

Terra Call uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and push notifications (firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
  • Twilio — SMS messaging services (twilio.com/legal/privacy)

11. SMS Messaging Program Details

Program Name: Terra Call Alerts

Program Description: Terra Call sends transactional emergency monitoring SMS alerts to caregivers when a linked care recipient misses a scheduled check-in or triggers an in-app emergency button. Messages are not promotional or marketing in nature.

How users opt in: Users opt in by creating a Terra Call account, providing their mobile phone number in their profile, and consenting to receive SMS alerts. Caregivers must additionally be linked to a care recipient who has granted them permission within the app.

Message frequency: Frequency varies based on care-recipient activity. A typical user receives 0–5 messages per day. Frequency may increase during an active emergency or repeated missed check-ins.

Message and data rates: Standard message and data rates may apply, set by your mobile carrier.

Opt out: Reply STOP to any Terra Call message at any time to immediately stop all SMS alerts. You may also remove your phone number from your profile in the app.

Help: Reply HELP to any Terra Call message, or email app@terra-call.com for assistance.

Carriers: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy and Terms: See this Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions.
