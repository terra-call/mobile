---
name: US-only App Store launch
description: Terra Call's storefront availability decision for the initial release.
---

Keep Terra Call available only in the United States for the initial App Store release. Do not automatically enable new storefronts.

**Why:** Current SMS delivery, regulatory posture, and support capacity are US-focused. International expansion needs country-by-country validation rather than an automatic global rollout.

**How to apply:** Preserve US-only availability during release and metadata work. Add another country only after confirming messaging reliability, privacy requirements, phone-number handling, and support readiness for that market.
