Here's why it's fine for now:

  • You're in development/testing — no real users yet
  • Spark plan covers everything you need for Weeks 1-6 easily

When to upgrade to Blaze (pay-as-you-go):

Bottom line:

  • Right now → Spark (free)
  • Week 7 before submission → upgrade to Blaze

Blaze only charges for what you use above the free tier, so if your user base starts small, your bill will likely be $0-5/month anyway.

Great question — here's the honest answer:

Push notifications themselves work in all countries. FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) is global. ✅

BUT — launching internationally has bigger requirements than just notifications:

Legal & Compliance

  • GDPR (Europe) — strict data privacy laws, requires consent screens, data deletion requests, privacy policy updates
  • PIPEDA (Canada) — similar to GDPR
  • PDPA (Southeast Asia) — Thailand, Singapore, etc.
  • Each country has its own health/medical app regulations
  • An app monitoring vulnerable people gets extra scrutiny

Technical

  • Language/localization — app needs to display in local languages
  • Time zones — check-in logic has to account for global time zones
  • SMS via Twilio — works internationally but costs more per message
  • Wyze cameras — not sold in every country

Business

  • Apple and Google Play both require country-specific compliance documentation
  • Some countries require a local business entity
  • Payment processing varies by country

My recommendation:
