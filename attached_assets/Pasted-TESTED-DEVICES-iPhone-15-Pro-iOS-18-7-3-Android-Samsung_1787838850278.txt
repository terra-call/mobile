TESTED DEVICES: iPhone 15 Pro (iOS 18.7.3). Android Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE (UI 8.5) tested extensively as the app is cross-platform.

APP DESCRIPTION & AUDIENCE: Terra Call is a family caregiving app. Care recipients (typically seniors living alone) tap one large "I'M OK" button daily to confirm they are safe; caregivers (family members or home-health aides) see a live dashboard of their loved ones' status. If a recipient taps "I'M NOT OK" or misses a check-in, every caregiver is alerted by push notification and SMS and can immediately start a video call. The problem solved: families currently rely on unanswered phone calls to know an aging parent is safe. Target audience: adult family caregivers and their aging relatives; also small home-care agencies.

SETUP & ACCESS: Two linked demo accounts are provided in App Review Information. Log in as review.recipient@terra-call.com / AppleReview2026!Cr to see the care recipient experience (check-in screen). Log in as review.caregiver@terra-call.com / AppleReview2026!Cg (second device or simulator) to see the caregiver dashboard. Tapping "I'M NOT OK" on the recipient account triggers an emergency alert and video-call flow on the caregiver account. The optional home-camera feature requires physical camera hardware (Reolink) and is not needed to evaluate the app. No sample files needed.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: Google Firebase (authentication, Firestore database, push notifications), Twilio Video (video calling), Twilio SMS (text alerts to caregivers with recipient consent), Google Maps (map of last check-in location). No payment processors (the app is entirely free with no in-app purchases), no AI services, no third-party data providers.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES: None. The app functions identically in all regions. It is currently offered in English only.
